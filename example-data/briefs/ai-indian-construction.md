# Brief: AI for Indian construction

> **Example.** This brief is the plugin's first test case. The run folder at `../runs/2026-11-20-ai-indian-construction/` holds realistic placeholder data, not real research.

**In one line:** Find the single best AI-led business for India's construction sector: a painful, paperwork- or labour-heavy workflow where a small team can win paying customers within 12 months.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Space** | Traditional industry adopting AI |
| **Playbook** | `india-traditional-ai` |
| **Industry** | Construction (building, infrastructure, real estate development) |
| **Use case** | Open: any workflow across bidding, site execution, billing, compliance or financing |
| **Geography** | India, starting with Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities |
| **Buyer** | Contractors, mid-size developers or lenders (research decides) |
| **User** | Site engineers, project managers, billing and compliance staff |
| **Business model** | B2B subscription or per-transaction fee |

## Constraints

- Small founding team, no hardware manufacturing.
- Must sell in under 6 months per customer.
- Interfaces in English, Hindi and at least one regional language.

## Success looks like

One idea with evidence of willingness to pay and a test that can run in 4 weeks.

## What we'll look for (from the playbook)

- Manual labour and paperwork: measurement books, running bills, site registers, WhatsApp photo trails.
- Compliance pain: RERA quarterly filings, labour cess, safety inspections, GST on works contracts.
- Money at stake: delayed and disputed bills, lender disbursement delays, cost overruns.
- New public rails: Account Aggregator and ULI for lending, GeM and e-procurement for tenders, Bhashini for language.
