# Brief: AI for traditional Indian industries

> **Example.** This brief is the plugin's first test case. The run folder at `../runs/2026-11-20-ai-traditional-indian-industries/` holds realistic placeholder data, not real research.

**In one line:** Find the traditional Indian industry where AI is the lowest-hanging fruit, then the single solution in it with the highest chance of success for a small team within 12 months.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Space** | Traditional industry adopting AI |
| **Playbook** | `india-traditional-ai` |
| **Scope** | Open: any traditional Indian industry (construction, logistics, manufacturing, retail, agriculture, healthcare, education, services, government) |
| **Industry** | Open: decided by the industry scan, not by this brief |
| **Use case** | Open: any workflow |
| **Geography** | India, starting with Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities |
| **Buyer** | Businesses or institutions in the chosen industry (research decides) |
| **Business model** | B2B subscription or per-transaction fee |

## Constraints

- Small founding team, no hardware manufacturing.
- Must sell in under 6 months per customer.
- Interfaces in English, Hindi and at least one regional language.

## Success looks like

One industry and one idea in it, with evidence of willingness to pay and a test that can run in 4 weeks.

## How the industry gets chosen

The industry scout screens 12-20 industries on the playbook's **Industry screen** rubric: pain and money at stake, digital readiness, buyer reachability and sales cycle, willingness to pay for software, competition density, why now (rules and rails), and regulatory friction. About three go forward to deep research. Ideas from all three compete in one top 10.

## What we'll look for (from the playbook)

- Manual labour and paperwork: bills, registers, WhatsApp photo trails, phone dispatch.
- Compliance pain: recurring filings, inspections, audits.
- Money at stake: delayed or disputed payments, working capital locked up, lost orders.
- New public rails: Account Aggregator and ULI for lending, ONDC, GeM and e-procurement, e-way bills and e-invoicing, Bhashini for language.
