2Target market

Complete

Small and mid-size contractors and developers doing ₹10–500 crore of work a year, plus the lenders who fund them. They run on WhatsApp, Excel and paper, and face growing compliance load from RERA, GST e-invoicing and digital government procurement.

Primary buyer
Owner or finance head at contractor/developer firms; technical head at housing finance companies
Primary user
Site engineers, QS/billing engineers, compliance executives
Where
India; pilots in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh
Stage
Early adopters already pay for basic site apps; AI layer largely absent

Segments

    Quarterly RERA progress filings, buyer updates, lender milestones. [E01, E02]

    Release construction-linked tranches after site inspections. [E11, E12]

5The pick

Complete

Top pick

Lender tranche inspections

Remote, AI-verified construction progress checks that let housing lenders release tranches faster and cheaper.

Why this one

It has the clearest payer: lenders already spend money on every stage inspection, the job recurs on every under-construction loan, and ULI gives a standard rail to plug into. Unlike contractor tools, it sells to a few dozen institutions rather than thousands of small firms. [E11, E12, E13, E16]

Riskiest assumption to test first

Lenders will accept geotagged photo evidence plus AI checks in place of (or before) a physical visit by an empanelled engineer.

How
Interview technical or credit heads at 8 housing finance companies, then run 20 inspections in parallel with their physical visits.
Success signal
3 lenders agree to a paid pilot; AI verdict matches the engineer's in 90% of cases
Cost
Under ₹1 lakh
Time
4 weeks
Why not the other survivors
  • RA bill autopilot: Strong pain, but thousands of small buyers and a long trust-building sale.
  • GeM/CPPP bid copilot: Real demand, but usage is episodic; better as a feature of the billing product.
