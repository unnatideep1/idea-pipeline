3Target market

Complete

Three shortlisted industries where paperwork blocks money: construction (contractors, developers and their lenders), freight trucking (small fleet owners and transporters) and MSME engineering suppliers. All run on WhatsApp, Excel and paper, and all already pay for some software.

Primary buyer
Owner or finance head at the business; technical or credit head at lenders
Primary user
Site and billing engineers, transport billing staff, costing and quality engineers
Where
India; pilots in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat and Uttar Pradesh
Stage
Early adopters pay for basic apps (site, GPS, accounting); the AI layer is largely absent

6The pick

Complete

Top pick · Construction (housing finance)

Lender tranche inspections

Remote, AI-verified construction progress checks that let housing lenders release tranches faster and cheaper.

Why this industry

Construction scored highest in the industry scan: money is visibly stuck behind paperwork and inspections, compliance recurs every quarter, and few AI products serve it. Within it, lenders are the easiest buyers: a few dozen institutions with budgets, not thousands of small contractors.

Why this solution

It has the clearest payer: lenders already spend money on every stage inspection, the job recurs on every under-construction loan, and ULI gives a standard rail to plug into. Unlike contractor tools, it sells to a few dozen institutions rather than thousands of small firms. [E11, E12, E13, E16]

How
